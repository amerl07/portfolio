Data & Tooling

Data Infrastructure & Tooling

Aerodynamics simulation results were scattered across individual spreadsheets, with no shared source of truth and no easy way to compare configurations across a season. I scoped a centralized workflow, defined the naming convention the team now uses to log every simulation, and directed development of an automated pipeline that turns new CFD uploads into a queryable dataset.

A companion web app lets teammates explore and compare simulations directly — pulling up scene renders, comparison graphs, and exportable tables in design reviews. I drove adoption through documentation and weekly walkthroughs, then expanded it based on ongoing feedback: cross-simulation comparison, new tracked parameters, and carbon-fiber mass estimation from CFD-derived geometry.

Cut post-processing time by ~85%.

Built for internal use by a 6-person aerodynamics subteam. The version linked below is a demo, built separately for portfolio and interview purposes — not the live internal tool.

Environmental Data

Environmental Data Analysis

A peer-reviewed book chapter analyzing global PM2.5 pollution patterns across a 12-year, multi-source dataset — satellite measurements, meteorological reanalysis, and socioeconomic indicators spanning 18 regions.

I engineered data pipelines (MATLAB, Python) to clean, standardize, and restructure the dataset, applying hierarchical clustering to expose non-linear spatiotemporal patterns, then built per-pollutant Random Forest regression models (scikit-learn) to isolate the anthropogenic and meteorological drivers behind them.
